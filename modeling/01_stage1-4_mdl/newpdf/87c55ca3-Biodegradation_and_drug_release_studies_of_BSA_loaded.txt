Abstract

  Certain variations in the process parameters (emulsification time, surfactant concentration) were performed in order
to prepare BSA-loaded gelatin microspheres with high loading efficacy and particle size ranging from 1 to 10 mm
using a procedure originally employed by Tabata and Ikada. The mathematical modelling of drug release in the
presence of collagenase showed a biphasic release pattern, where the rate constant for the initial time release
confirmed the influence of the particle size and/or enzymatic degradation rate on drug release rate. © 2002 Elsevier
Science B.V. All rights reserved.
